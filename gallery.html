TX16S MK3

On your radio

Wallpapers

Copy to /IMAGES/Wallpapers/. Open Appearance → Wallpaper & Startup and apply the background. Select Transparent in Dashboard options and turn off shared theme matching.

Model images

Copy the full-size PNG to /IMAGES/. KSE Touch looks up the connected aircraft by name. Keep its filename matched to that aircraft. A smaller thumbnail is also available for the EdgeTX model picker.

Startup screens

Copy to /IMAGES/Wallpapers/. Under Appearance → Wallpaper & Startup, select the startup image and apply it for the next power-on.
